# Known limitations

[User guide](README.md) › Known limitations

- **Only the standard checkout.** The pickup point picker works with Magento's standard
  (Luma-based) checkout and the multi-address checkout. Other checkouts, such as Hyvä or one-step
  checkout modules, aren't supported.
- **One Polkurier account per installation.** All websites share it.
